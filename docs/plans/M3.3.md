## 5. New mission: the island airport (draft, research started)
**Facts so far** (to go into URBAN_RESEARCH with full citations before any blurb is written):
- **March 2026:** the Premier said the province would take over the City of Toronto's stake in Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport to extend the runways for some jets. Mayor Chow opposed jets downtown over noise ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/ford-billy-bishop-airport-take-years-9.7125053), [Wings Magazine](https://www.wingsmagazine.com/ontario-to-take-control-of-torontos-stake-in-city-airport-lands-ford/)).
- **April 2026:** the province said it intended to expropriate about a third of **Little Norway Park** for the expansion. On 23 April, City Council voted to contest it ([Wikipedia: Little Norway Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Norway_Park)).
- **May 2026:** the province passed **Bill 110, the Building Billy Bishop Airport Act.** It lets the province take over the city's role and lands. The Premier said the park and the islands won't be developed, but the law gives the power ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/ford-ontario-little-norway-park-billy-bishop-airport-expansion-9.7187067), [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/canada/article-ontario-introduces-promised-bill-to-take-torontos-stake-in-billy/), [Ontario Construction News](https://ontarioconstructionnews.com/ford-says-little-norway-park-wont-be-developed-as-province-advances-billy-bishop-airport-expansion-legislation)).
- **July 2026:** Council demanded the act's repeal and the land's return ([National Observer](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/07/29/news/toronto-city-council-passes-motion-demanding-ford-repeal-billy-bishop-act-return)). PortsToronto has put the expansion at up to $5 billion. *That figure is reported second-hand; verify it before use.*
- **To check:** whether anything newer than July 2026 exists, the Tripartite Agreement (no jets, expires 2033), and noise evidence for jets near homes. Use sources only, no invented numbers.

**Mission draft: "Runway Rumble"** (Waterfront, foot of Bathurst; working title)
- **Story (fiction, labelled):** [NEW NAME] declares "JETS DOWNTOWN!" and sends rats to stake out a runway through Little Norway Park's playground and ball diamond. The blurb says plainly that in reality the province says it won't develop the park, but the law allows it.
- **Beats:**
  1. Ride the Eireann Quay approach while planes land overhead, left to right.
  2. Defend the park: pull out survey stakes while **Baggage Rats** roll suitcases along the ground.
  3. Boss, the **Jet Tug Rat:** a baggage tug pulling a private jet. It taxis across, its jet blast pushes you back (no damage, but it pushes you toward hazards), and it stalls to refuel. Hit the tug then, and stomp the cockpit.
- **Livability upgrade:** the park stays a park; quiet island airport.
- **Map:** the airport ferry and tunnel pavilion at the foot of Bathurst on the Waterfront map, with the island airport visible across the gap.
