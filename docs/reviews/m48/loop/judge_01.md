Scale: ref 03's craft = 9 to 10. Baseline (judge_00): raccoon 4.0, Dog Fjord 3.5.

- **Raccoon:** the heads jumped from about 4 to about 6.5 in all three. The fur breaks, tufted ears with a pale
  fringe, the white brow tufts, the 4-tone amber iris with glint and lid rim, the whiskers, the wet nose: this is the
  first time the art looks like pixel art and not vector art. The bodies are still about 3.5 to 4 (tubes, mittens,
  flat rings), and they drag the total down.
- **Dog Fjord:** +0.5 for the iter_04 head and a richer skin ramp. Otherwise it is the same face in all three
  variants, and it is the weakest drawing in the set now.

## Ranking and the direction

Ranked by **where the art should go**, not by current finish:

1. **B: the palette, the costume and the attitude.** The warm taupe coat (brown-violet shadows, cream lights) is the
   most charming colour on the page and the nearest to refs 04/05. The diagonal pump across the chest is the only
   pose with real attitude ("come at me"). A diagonal axis is also a strong, clean straight line. The green knit
   scarf is the ref-03 note. Its drawing is the muddiest of the three (see below), but these are fixable drawing
   problems, not direction problems.
2. **C: the composition.** About a 1.22x crop with the head filling the frame is exactly ref 03's proportion. More
   pixels on the face means more room for craft, and the head in C is the best-rendered raccoon head of the round
   (the mask's lighter upper band, the bigger eyes, the lit side losing its outline). The cap is a great character
   note. But the level pump reads as a **barbell or a railing**, not a floor pump (both ends are vertical bars), and
   everything below the rail is weak.
3. **A: the finish, but the safest.** It is the best-finished whole, and its pump is the most convincing object.
   But it is the baseline's pose redrawn: the pump stands beside it like a display, the hand is on the hip, and the
   body is a floating ball. Ivan explicitly asked not to redraw the same thing, so A should not carry the direction.

**Chosen direction for round 02: B's warm coat and swagger pose, framed at C's close crop, finished to A's level.**
- Scale the figure up about 1.15 to 1.25x so the head is the hero (as in C). The diagonal pump then runs from the
  T-handle by the far ear down across the chest and out at the lower right, and it and the paws cover the weak
  lower torso. A finished bottom comes from the pump foot, the forearms and the scarf: no floating ball, no tail
  needed (crop it, or show only a tufted tip behind the hip).
- Keep the green scarf, but resolve the colour clash: the teal pump next to the green scarf makes two similar cool
  hues fight. Round 02 should try at least two accent sets on the warm coat:
  - (i) green scarf + red-orange or cherry pump (complementary, ref 03 + a hot pump);
  - (ii) red bandana (A's, which works) + teal pump (B's).
- Carry the **cycling cap** from C into one round-02 variant (on the warm coat). It adds character; it needs a
  rounder crown and a brim with 3 tones, not a flat red parallelogram.

### Dog Fjord: rounds 02 and 03 must explore the face, not the suit
All three variants share one face, and that is the biggest remaining gap in the whole set. Suits: take **C's broad
square shoulders** (the best bust shape, a bulldog in a big suit) with **B's tie idea** (gold tie with little red
cars: the best satire) or A's red tie, plus a flag or gold pin. The suit reaching the frame bottom is fine.

## What improved (since round 00)
- Palette ramps added: 9-step cool fur, 9-step warm coat, 8-step dog skin, 4-step iris. Shadows now shift toward
  blue-violet.
- Fur is drawn: `furBreak` boundaries, tufted silhouettes, fringed ears, white brow tufts breaking the head line.
- Selective coloured silhouette: there are no more black lines between regions on the raccoon.
- Eyes: dark-top/bright-bottom iris, pupil, square glint, wet sparkle, lid rim, pale lower rim. They now are the
  focal point.
- The pump is straight in all three (checked below).
- Dog Fjord's head is now the iter_04 silhouette, and his nose has a 4-tone wet look.

## What still separates it from ref 03 (ranked)

1. **Dog Fjord's face is unmodelled.** It is the same in all variants. Specifics on the A close-up:
   - **Wrinkles are still 1 px rose lines**: the two forehead arcs, the nasolabial curve from the nose wing round the
     grin corner on each side, the vertical philtrum line, and the chin arc. At 112 the forehead lines become 2 or 3
     horizontal scratches.
   - **The muzzle has no mass.** In a bulldog, the upper lips (the flews) are two fat lobes that hang over the teeth.
     Here the lip is a thin line above a white crescent. There is no lit top plane on the muzzle, no core shadow
     under it, and no cast shadow of the lip on the teeth.
   - **The jowls are lines, not masses.** The big curve at the right cheek is a stroke. The weight should be a lower
     mass that sags over the collar: lit on top, a dark crease under it, reflected light at the bottom edge.
   - **The forehead is one flat tone** (the artist says so too), with a pillow-shaded rim around the head.
   - **The patch eye** is better (an amber iris now, readable at 112), but the patch is still 2 flat browns plus one
     light chip. It needs a chestnut ramp, a fur-break edge into the pink, and a socket shadow.
   - **The whisker dots** (6 dots) are orphan specks; drop them or make them 2-pixel pore clusters with a lit edge.
   - **The grin teeth** are a white crescent with tick lines: no gum, no shading toward the corners, no lip shadow.
   - The brows are still flat black bars.
2. **Raccoon paws are mittens.** A: dark slate blocks with 3 to 4 grey rib lines, a ribbed "glove" round the
   barrel, and the hip paw a lump with 2 notches. B: two ribbed mitts on the shaft. C: two "stamp" blocks with
   vertical ribs sitting on top of the barrel instead of wrapping round it. A raccoon hand has 5 long fingers, a
   palm pad and visible knuckles. Draw fingers wrapping round the tube (the far side hidden, knuckle highlights on
   top) and a lighter grey-brown palm, with fur tufts at the wrist.
3. **Arms are tubes.** A's upper arms are cylinders with a hard banded light stripe and a flat round cap where the
   elbow should be, and the far hand-on-hip arm reads like a bent pipe. In B the far arm doesn't separate from the
   chest: there is a flat taupe trapezoid with grey seams and a stray dark notch at the centre of the chest, below
   the scarf. Arms need taper (shoulder, elbow, a thinner wrist), fur tufts on the silhouette, and a contact shadow
   where an arm crosses the body.
4. **The mask is still one dark mass.** C is best: it has a lighter upper band. A and B are a near-black plane with
   one lighter strip under the eye. Ref 03's mask has a sheen across the brow ridge and the cheekbone, its deepest
   black at the inner eye corners and around the socket, and soft fur breaks on its lower edge. The forehead stripe
   still ends in a smooth vector V on all three (A at the top centre of the head, C under the cap).
5. **Body masses and the tail.** A's body is a fluffy ball with evenly spaced vertical tick strokes on the belly.
   Its tail rings are flat dark "rubber bands" painted straight across, not wrapping the cylinder, and the tail tip
   is a dark flat chunk. B's lower torso is a soft blob with the hose looping under it. C's belly below the rail is
   a fringe of identical spikes. With the close crop, most of this can leave the frame. What stays needs a modelled
   chest ruff, not even strokes.
6. **Pump as an object.**
   - A is the best: a straight vertical axis, a yellow barrel with a lit stripe and an orange core, chrome caps, a
     T-foot plate, and a gauge with a needle. It lacks a crisp specular glint on the chrome, and its foot is a flat
     slab with no pegs.
   - B: the gauge (at about 62% up, beside the shoulder) is drawn edge-on as a white loop, so it **reads as a hook**.
     The bottom cap and foot read as a grey squeegee blade. The rod and handle are fine.
   - C: as said, a barbell: the foot is a vertical dark plank at the right with the hose looping under it.
7. **Small details from ref 03 not yet used:** light catching the fur tips on the lit rim (top of the head and ears),
   the inner ear pinkish-grey ramp, and a nose bridge highlight line.
8. **Clusters:** some straight horizontal banding remains (the muzzle highlight in A is a long flat strip, and the
   cap brim in C has stepped parallel bands). The `furStrokes` sometimes make evenly spaced parallel ticks (the A
   belly, the C belly): vary length and spacing more.

## Top 5 fixes for round 02

1. **Dog Fjord: real exploration of his face, with 2 or 3 genuinely different renderings inside the iter_04
   silhouette.** Each one needs:
   - a modelled muzzle: two fat flews with a lit top plane, a core shadow, and a cast shadow on the teeth;
   - jowls as hanging masses over the collar;
   - wrinkles as shadow shapes with a lit ridge above, never 1 px lines;
   - a forehead dome with a warm highlight;
   - the patch in a chestnut ramp with a fur edge;
   - a bigger patch eye with a white, an iris and a glint;
   - a nose with a blue-grey sheen and nostril shapes;
   - a grin with gum and corner shading.

   Also try a different bust angle (a slight three-quarter turn toward screen-left, chin up) or head sunk into C's
   broad shoulders. Below about `c.S` 0.6, drop the fold lines and dots entirely.
2. **The raccoon on the chosen direction: B's warm coat + diagonal pump at C's crop.** Redraw the body under it: arms
   that taper with fur on the silhouette and a contact shadow across the chest, and no flat trapezoid or notch.
   Try two accent sets (a green scarf + red pump, a red bandana + teal pump) and one cap variant.
3. **Real raccoon hands.** Five long dark fingers wrapping the tube, knuckle highlights, a lighter palm pad visible on
   one hand, fur at the wrist. No mittens, ribs or stamps.
4. **The mask modelled in 3 or 4 tones.** A sheen across the brow ridge and the cheekbone, the deepest black around
   the eye sockets, fur breaks on the lower edge. End the forehead stripe in fur strokes, not a smooth V.
5. **The pump as a convincing object.** The gauge face turned toward the viewer as a round dial, never edge-on. A
   chrome specular glint and reflected light on the rod and caps. A foot plate with two pegs, a hose ferrule. The
   colour chosen as the accent, not competing with the face.

## Keep, don't touch
- The raccoon head rendering approach: `furBreak` boundaries, tufted silhouettes, fringed ears with dark-streaked
  insides, white brow tufts, the 4-tone iris with glint, wet sparkle and lid rim, the whiskers, the wet nose.
- The selective, coloured silhouette system (dark on the shadow side, softer on the lit side, coloured per material).
- B's warm taupe ramp (`@ # $ I % & J * +`) and the dog skin ramp keys.
- `pumpAxis()`: the straight-by-construction pump. Use it for every pump from now on.
- A's red bandana drawing (its knot and fold shading are good) as an accessory option.
- Dog Fjord: the iter_04 head silhouette, the hair strand clusters, the three expressions, and the amber patch eye
  that reads at 112.
