# M4.4 Review: your notes on M4.2 / M4.3

**Play it:** https://besit.github.io/trashpanda/. Add `?debug` for **SELECT MISSION**.

Everything from your list is in. Numbers in brackets refer to the gallery at the bottom.

## The posters (folder for review: `docs/reviews/m44/posters/`)
- **Why they looked bad:** the posters were drawn at the game's own size (384 x 216) and blown up 5x. The saved file was sharp; the preview was the game canvas shrinking it back down, so the words broke up. On the tall poster the link was squeezed to fit, which made it unreadable.
- **Now:**
  - Posters are drawn at **768 x 432**, twice the game's resolution. Street scenes use the game's pixels at 2x; the portraits and all the words use the finer grid.
  - They're saved at **2304 x 1296** with hard pixels; we do the upscale, so nothing gets smoothed.
  - The preview on the share screen is the saved image itself, shown at your screen's full resolution (6). The words are readable in the preview now.
  - **PLAY FREE** has its own line on the tall posters, with the link under it.
- **A hero portrait of the raccoon**, made like Dog Fjord's:
  - grey fur, the bandit mask with a determined slant
  - a red bandana and the yellow pump over its shoulder
  - two faces: a smirk and a fighting yell

  Dog Fjord is re-drawn facing left for the VS layouts.
- **Six new posters**, each a nod to a classic game screen (an homage to the layout, nothing copied):
  1. **VERSUS:** a fighting game's VS screen. The two of them in opposite corners, a slash between, VS in a burst.
  2. **TITLE:** a 16-bit title screen. The raccoon bursts out of a giant bike wheel, the name on a ribbon, PRESS START.
  3. **SUNSET:** an arcade racer. A striped sun setting over the lake, a road with a protected bike lane on each side running to the horizon, the raccoon riding off into it.
  4. **BOSSES:** a boss-select grid. You're in the middle; the bosses you've beaten are lit and the rest are shadows marked ???, with Dog Fjord last. It changes as you play.
  5. **STREETS:** a beat-em-up cover. A night street, the raccoon up front, the rats coming, Dog Fjord looming in the sky.
  6. **SCORES:** an arcade high-score table, your run among this device's best (real scores only).
- **Your fixes to the old four:**
  - **Queen:** the raccoon is 1.5x, lower and nearer the camera, no longer bigger than the streetcar. On the tall one the streetcar comes in from the right and doesn't touch the raccoon.
  - **Face-off:** Dog Fjord looks left, and the VS has a burst behind it.
  - **Bike bus:** two rows of riders drawn back to front, the raccoon in front of everyone.
  - **Lake:** the raccoon is on the third of the picture, and the boats are in the water.
- **Which ones to keep is up to you.** All ten are in the game now (< > on the share screen). Tell me which to drop, reorder or change.

## Global
- **Initials with a keyboard:** just type them, and Backspace takes a letter back (1). The arrow keys still work.
- **Initials on a phone:** tapping the letters brings up the phone's own keyboard, and Go starts the run (2). On phones the panel sits at the top, so the keyboard doesn't cover it.
- **The city map's arrows:**
  - The left and right labels now carry a `^` turned with the text, so it points out of the map (3).
  - The font had no `^` at all, so the labels above the map had no arrow either; they do now.
- **Bike share bikes are orange:** the docks, the trailer and the riders in traffic (4).
- **The share screen after Save the Science:** it only appears in the payoff after a mission's **first** win (and from the pause menu). A replay, or a mission from the debug list, skips the payoff and the share button with it; that's most likely what happened. The button was also easy to miss, so it's now a blinking boxed **SHARE A POSTER** (5).
- **The latest neighbourhoods:**
  - `docs/maps/` has every district at full scale as it starts.
  - `docs/maps/after/` has them with every improvement and the whole bike network.

## Save the Science
- **The guards walk their beat**, back and forth across a crate. Crouch behind the crate and the guard walks straight past you (7). Once its back is turned, hit it from behind: a one-hit **TAKEDOWN** (8).
  - Caught in the beam, or hitting it from the front, raises the alarm as before.
  - Jumping over a guard while it faces you still gets you seen.
- **The Wrecker:**
  - **Only hittable while its ball is stuck** in the ground after a drop, for about 3 seconds (9). The crane shimmers while it's open; a hit any other time clangs off and says "CLANG! WAIT FOR THE DROP".
  - At the end it strains and shouts **GET CLEAR!**, hauls the ball out, swings it up to the far end of its arc and **swings twice**.
  - **Every swing can be jumped.** It always starts from that high end, above a standing raccoon (10), and you jump it at the low point or stand clear of the arc.
  - The old swing could start right on top of you, with the chain dropping onto your head. That was the hit you couldn't avoid.
  - The shockwaves from a drop still have to be jumped.
  - It takes about three windows to beat (24 HP).

## Districts
- **The CN Tower** is the whole tower now: trefoil base, the shaft, the main pod with its window band, the SkyPod and the antenna (12). Before, only the base and shaft showed; the rest ran off the top of the map.
- **The Waterfront:** riding west on Lake Shore or Queens Quay takes you to High Park, like the trail does (15).
- **The Freedom Arches** overlap left over right, so each arch's near leg passes in front of the next one (13).
