## The Bike Bus (new mission, East End, on the map)
- **What it is.** The briefing explains it in the raccoon's words: a bike bus runs like a school bus on bikes, with a set route and stops along the way; kids wait at the stops, a grown-up leads, and everyone rides to school together (15). Dog Fjord wants the bike lane for drop-off parking.
- **How it plays.** It's the first mission played on the map itself. From Broadview + Dundas, lead the bus along Dundas, up Carlaw, along Gerrard and down Leslie to a school on Dundas (10). At each of the four stops (yellow BIKE BUS signs), stop beside the kids: they hop on and ride in a line behind you (12). Three SUVs are parked in the bike lane; ring your bell (E) at each one and it drives off (11). An arrow always points to the next thing to do, and the bell is on a 75-second clock. Late: THE BELL RANG, back to the first stop (13).
- **Payoff: a School Street.** Barriers at both ends of the block outside the school, full bike racks (14). The upgrade is A SCHOOL STREET (safety, cycling, walking).
- **The research** ([URBAN_RESEARCH §24](../URBAN_RESEARCH.md)): how bike buses work (Green Communities Canada), Toronto parents running one since April 2023 (Toronto Star), and the 2019 Mountview Ave School Street pilot outside Keele Street PS (8 80 Cities). The East York bike bus Instagram you sent is noted as the inspiration; the game names no real group.
- **It now comes before Death by Highway**, so the finale stays last. Twelve missions in all.

## Share posters
- **Where.** After every mission, the evidence box under the payoff has a small **[ S: SHARE ]** button (tap it on a phone). The pause menu has **SHARE YOUR CITY** any time.
- **What.** Four poster designs, each in **wide** (1920×1080, for posts) and **tall** (1080×1920, for stories): Queen St with the raccoon and a streetcar, the face-off with Dog Fjord, the bike bus with the kids, and the lake with the coloured sailboats (20, 21, 22). They're drawn from the game's own art at native resolution and upscaled with hard pixels. Each one carries the title, **I JUST WON: (mission)** or MY TORONTO SO FAR, your **Livability, score and missions**, and the game's address.
- **How.** On a phone, X, FACEBOOK and INSTAGRAM open the system share sheet with the image attached. On a computer: X opens a post with the text and link, Facebook opens its sharer, and the poster is saved for you to attach. Instagram has no web share link, so it saves the poster to post from the app. SAVE just saves it.
