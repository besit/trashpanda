**Play it:** https://besit.github.io/trashpanda/ (desktop, or a phone in landscape). Add `?debug` and the title menu gets **SELECT MISSION** (any mission, briefing first) and **WATCH VIGNETTES**.

**CONTINUE** keeps your save. Unlocks:
- **The Lane Eater** opens after *Slow Down, Dog*.
- **The Great Parking Lot** and **The Waterfront Heist** open after *King of the Street*.

On a fresh save, use SELECT MISSION.

This review covers two passes: **M2.1** (your M2 notes, shipped as tag `m2.1`) and **M3** (new content).

## New in M3
**Two new districts:**
- **East End**, east of Downtown along Queen or Dundas (10–13):
  - The **Don Valley**: river, trail and DVP, crossed on bridges. The Queen St bridge inscription is a plaque.
  - **Broadview** and the Broadview Hotel, Riverdale Park East, East Chinatown, Leslieville.
  - **MEGAMART**'s parking lot (fictional).
- **Waterfront**, the first district you reach by riding **south**: take Spadina, University or Bay off the bottom of Downtown (14–18).
  - The Gardiner overhead, Lake Shore beneath it.
  - The CN Tower and the stadium in Roundhouse Park.
  - Queens Quay's streetcar in its grassy median (streetcars only).
  - The Martin Goodman Trail, the boardwalk, Sugar Beach, sailboats.
  - Out west: the Cinesphere, the pods, and Dog Fraud's fictional **MEGA SPA**, fenced off behind a locked gate.

**Three new missions:**
- **5. The Lane Eater** (High Park, Bloor West) (20, 21):
  - The screen scrolls on its own while a paving machine eats the bike lane behind you.
  - **Ring your bell** (E) at cyclists to grow a peloton. Each rider takes a hit for you and slows the machine.
  - At the end, the machine turns into a boss with three weak points: the conveyor (jump attacks), the cab (jump attacks), and the grinder, which is only open for 2 s after it lunges and **overheats**.
- **7. The Great Parking Lot** (East End) (22, 23):
  - Waves, then a locked arena: **pump five cracked asphalt patches** into saplings while rats defend them.
  - Boss: **the Monster SUV**. It charges and stalls at the edge. Stomp its roof, and pump its tyres while it's stalled.
  - Payoff: the lot becomes a park.
- **8. The Waterfront Heist** (Waterfront) (24, 25):
  - A new rat, **the Lobbyist**: he never fights, he hangs back, and every rat near him glints gold and takes **half damage**. Corner him first.
  - Then pump down four MEGA SPA fence panels across the promenade.
  - Payoff: the fences and the gate go, and the island gets a public beach.

Each mission has a before/after vignette (30–33) and a sourced blurb: the Bloor lanes (§1, §19), parking minimums (§6), Ontario Place (§7). The MEGA SPA is fictional and the blurb says so; it never names Therme.

## Known issues / notes
- **Difficulty:** my bot wins M8 without taking a hit, but I suspect real players will find the lobbyists add more. M7 cost the bot 2 lives. In M5 the bot can't do jump attacks, so I verified the conveyor and cab by direct hits; that fight is probably the hardest so far. Your read on all three is the most useful thing here.
- **First load is about 2.3 MB** (budget 2.5). All five maps' JSON loads at boot for the City Map; I'll make that lazy in M4 before the North district lands (PERFORMANCE.md).
- Some plaques in the new districts use well-known facts not yet checked against a primary source (URBAN_RESEARCH §20, marked 🟡).
