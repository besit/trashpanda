# M3.1 Review: your M3 notes

**Play it:** https://besit.github.io/trashpanda/ (desktop, or a phone in landscape). Add `?debug` to get **SELECT MISSION** and **WATCH VIGNETTES** on the title screen.

**New game vs. continue:**
- **NEW GAME** now asks for a difficulty, then your initials.
- **CONTINUE** keeps an M3 save. It becomes a NORMAL run that starts scoring from 0.

**See every map without riding around:** full-scale images of all five districts are in [docs/maps/](../maps/), with traffic and people included.

Every visual change below has a screenshot, so you can check it without replaying a mission. Numbers in brackets refer to the gallery at the end.

I checked the full-scale maps but haven't ridden every new gap; tell me if one looks wrong or a hidden heart got too easy.
