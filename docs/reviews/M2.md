## New in M2
**Two new districts.** Ride off the map edge along Queen or Dundas to cross.
- **High Park** (west):
  - Grenadier Pond with cherry trees, oak savanna, car-free park roads
  - the zoo's **capybaras** (read their plaque), the HIGH PARK sign
  - **Parkside Drive**'s school zone and its cut-down speed camera
  - Roncesvalles with the streetcar and the **Revue**, and Bloor with its bike lanes
- **Downtown** (east):
  - **Nathan Phillips Square**: City Hall, the TORONTO sign, the arches over the pool
  - Old City Hall, Osgoode Hall
  - **Chinatown**'s lanterns, **Graffiti Alley** (moved here from Queen West, as you noted)
  - the news van bursting out of 299 Queen W
  - King West warehouses turning into glass towers
- The **city map** (M) names the district and shows the city west to east with open missions per district.

**Three new missions** (unlocked after The Bike Lane War):
- **Let the Streetcar Through** (Queen West): no fighting, all timing. Get the 501 to the end on time.
  - Pump the yellow call box on red signals for transit priority.
  - Move a double-parked van and a left-turner off the tracks.
  - Swat the cones the rats drop.
- **King of the Street** (Downtown, King St):
  - **SUV Rats** drive through your lane. Jump on the roof or pump the bumper (each hit stalls the car) until it gives up: "RIGHT TURN ONLY!"
  - The boss is the **Bureaucrat Rat**: stamp volleys you can swat, a clipboard shove, summoned clerks, and red tape you jump.
  - Dog Fraud appears in the world for the first time, at his podium.
- **Slow Down, Dog** (High Park, Parkside): the real camera story (cut down 7 times, once thrown in a pond).
  - Chase the rats hauling it to the pond: boost in bursts and jump the cones.
  - Then **build** a speed hump, a raised crosswalk and a curb extension while SUV Rats speed through. Every piece you finish slows the cars.

Each mission has a before/after vignette and a map payoff:
- **Queen:** streetcars get priority at the lights.
- **King:** red transit lanes and patios where the parked cars were.
- **Parkside:** humps, a raised crosswalk, SLOW stencils, the camera back up, and traffic at half speed.

Livability now goes 60 → 74 across four missions.

## Questions
1. **The district seams:** riding off the map edge plays a short wipe and loads the next district (one district in memory at a time, so phones stay fast). Is that OK, or do you want one seamless map? That would cost download size and memory.
2. **Speed cameras:** in *Slow Down, Dog* the camera goes back up in the payoff, even though Ontario's real ban stands. The main fix is the street design. Keep the camera in the payoff, or design-only?
3. **Next:** M3 is missions 5–10 (including The Lane Eater on Bloor), the North, East End and Waterfront districts, the Lobbyist and the Mega Rat. Or another polish pass on M2 first?
