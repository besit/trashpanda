# M4.3 Review: bike share, a growing bike network, and the code + game review

**Play it:** https://besit.github.io/trashpanda/. Add `?debug` for **SELECT MISSION** (now 13 missions, each with your best rank).

Your four notes are all in, plus everything you approved from the reviews. Numbers in brackets refer to the gallery at the bottom.

## Your notes
- **Bike share: the Dock Drop** (Downtown, opens after King of the Street; mission 9 of 13). A mission played on the map, like the Bike Bus. The bikes are unbranded: green step-throughs with a basket.
  - You tow a trailer of docks to six gaps: Chinatown, City Hall, Old City Hall, Graffiti Alley, King + John and King + Bay. Stop at a yellow gap for a moment and a station goes down; two share bikes ride straight out of it (10, 11).
  - Dog Fjord calls the stations "clutter". His crew (rats in hard hats) come for three of the new ones and cart them off; ring your bell near one and it drops the station and runs (12). A station that gets carted all the way off has to be set again. You have 90 seconds.
  - The open gaps and the crew show on the city map (13).
  - **Afterwards** there are stations on every map (two or three per district) and a third of the cyclists ride share bikes (14).
  - The payoff card has the real numbers: started in 2011; in 2025, 7.8 million trips, 180 new stations (its biggest year) and all 25 wards (City of Toronto, 2025 Cycling Year in Review).
- **Strachan has painted bike lanes** from the start, in Queen West and down to the lake, with cyclists in them (1). That matches reality south of King; the game runs them up to Queen. The Waterfront Heist makes them protected.
- **Your call on Livability:** the tier names are gone, "Amsterdam/Copenhagen territory" included.
  - The HUD shows **LIVABILITY n/100** and, under it, **BIKE NETWORK n/28** (1).
  - **Every mission adds two or three streets to the bike network**, anywhere in the city: a protected lane on the main streets, or a **slow street** on the side streets. A slow street has humps and sharrows, half the cars at cycling speed, and bikes take the lane (6–9).
  - After a mission, its streets on the map you're on sweep in with the rest of the payoff, and a line names all of them: "BIKE NETWORK 3/28 STREETS / NEW: QUEEN ST W" (3).
  - The city map draws the network over the streets: green for protected, yellow for slow, grey for painted. A legend row under the map explains the colours (2, 4, 5).
  - Mission 13 finishes it. The report card says **EVERY STREET: SAFE TO RIDE** and counts protected (22) and slow (6) streets (20).
  - Which mission adds which street is in `src/data/network.ts`. For example, the Bike Lane War does Queen across Queen West and Downtown, the Bike Bus does Broadview and Dundas E, and Death by Highway does Lake Shore and Dundas W.
  - I simplified what each street is like today. Bloor, University and Roncesvalles really do have lanes of some kind, but the game starts them without (URBAN_RESEARCH §26 says so).

## Code review
I reviewed M4.2's code and fixed all ten findings:
- **A crash on phones:** tapping the city map while another district was LOADING hit a destroyed label.
- **Overlapping labels:** long neighbour names on the city map could stick out into the title row or under the close button. They're clamped by their own length now.
- **The hurt blink:** in Save the Science, hiding behind a crate hid the blink that shows you're invulnerable.
- **Arena spots:** pre-placed ones (fences, stakes, rail piles) could sit a few pixels off the locked screen. The arena now locks where they expect it.
- **Pop-ins I'd missed:**
  - The guard's second backup rat appeared on screen.
  - The Jet Tug's nose jumped into place before revving; it now rolls in.
  - The Wrecker's ball snapped sideways at the start of each swing.
- **The Highway Mech's slabs** were solid in mid-air before they landed.
- **Late hints:** a delayed hint could still land on the win/lose card.
- **The Lane Eater** redrew its whole paved strip every frame, growing with distance; now only the stretch on screen.

## Game review
These are the changes you approved:
- **Boss checkpoint:** once a boss fight starts, losing a life brings you back to the boss, not the start of the street (15). The Lane Eater keeps the full restart, because its boss is the end of the chase.
- **Mission ranks:** S/A/B/C when you win (16), and the best is kept. It shows on FIXED markers (17) and in SELECT MISSION.
  - Fights rank on hits taken, counting the lives lost along the way. The streetcar escort ranks on how late the 501 runs; the map missions on the time left.
  - Replays still earn no points, but they can improve your rank.
- **Hit feedback:** I need to correct my question. Your hits already had hit-stop, a flash and shake. What was missing was a clear impact, so there's now an impact star where each hit lands (bigger on the third hit of the combo and on stomps) and a swoosh showing the swing's reach (18).
- **Small fixes I said I'd make anyway:**
  - **The Megamart wall** is three different bays (the store, a garden centre, a pickup bay with SUV DAYS: FREE PARKING) instead of the same sign every screen (19).
  - **Text on text:**
    - A dark band now sits behind the top HUD line, the GO / BOSS! banners, the win and lose cards, and the report card.
    - The Bike Bus and Dock Drop status lines sit in their own box under the Livability box; they used to run into it.
    - Hint boxes are more opaque.
    - The off-screen arrow's word (KIDS, DOCK, RAT) has a backing.
  - **The Wrecker hint** is back to two lines.

## Worth checking
- **The Dock Drop's 90 seconds:** I estimate about 60 s of riding plus the rats. Is it tense enough without being stressful?
- **The rank thresholds:** S means no hits, A up to 2, B up to 5. Possibly too strict on Hard.
- **The network colours on the city map**, and whether the slow streets (Ossington, Bellwoods, John, Carlaw, Parkside, King) read as different from protected lanes on the street itself.

## Numbers
- 57 browser tests (4 new) and 21 unit tests pass.
- The new unit tests check that every street on the network is on its map with a patch, and that 13 missions add up to exactly 100.
- JS is 384 KB gzipped.
